About Jordan Tupper

Jordan Tupper is a Baton Rouge, Louisiana-based musician, bassist, vocalist, music educator, program leader and community-focused professional. His career spans more than 19 years across education, live performance, program development, team leadership, communications, digital marketing and community engagement.

His work has consistently centered on building programs and bringing people together. In education, Jordan has led school-wide music programs, directed instrumental ensembles, recruited and developed students, coordinated large events and worked with administrators, families and community partners. In music, he continues to perform throughout south Louisiana on bass guitar, upright bass and vocals.

Jordan Tupper earned a bachelor's degree in music and religious studies from Elon University and a master's degree in music education from Austin Peay State University. He also completed a Digital Marketing Science Certificate through Louisiana State University Online.

Music and live performance

Jordan Tupper is an active Baton Rouge musician performing across jazz, classic rock, funk, soul, contemporary covers and live event music. His current projects include Night Hog, Cover 6 Band and Standley, Tupper & Petersen.

With Night Hog and Cover 6, Jordan performs bass and vocals for clubs, restaurants, festivals, private parties and community events. With Standley, Tupper & Petersen, he plays upright bass in a Baton Rouge jazz trio featuring guitarist Jim Standley and tenor saxophonist Glen Petersen. The trio's work was featured by 225 Magazine in 2026.

Performance videos, recordings, upcoming appearances and additional music information are available at JordanTupperMusic.com.

Education, programs and leadership

Jordan Tupper's professional background includes nearly two decades of work in schools and educational programs in Louisiana and Tennessee. From 2021 through 2025, he served as a music educator at The Episcopal School of Baton Rouge, designing and managing school-wide programming serving more than 450 students. Earlier roles included music and band leadership at Port Allen High and Middle Schools, The Dunham School, Pope John Paul II High School and other educational settings.

His work as a program leader has included strategic planning, recruiting and retention, team leadership, project management, operations coordination, stakeholder engagement, technology integration and process improvement. One of his earlier secondary music programs grew from approximately 10 students to more than 100 through recruitment, program redesign and community-focused leadership.

Jordan's more recent professional work has also extended into website development, branding, digital marketing and small-business operations. That work complements his background in education and music by combining communications, technology and practical program management.

Recognition and community involvement

Jordan Tupper was named a national quarterfinalist for the 2024 Music Educator Award, presented by the Recording Academy and GRAMMY Museum. Austin Peay State University also profiled Jordan and fellow alumnus Vanessa Cobb after their selection from more than 2,000 initial nominees nationwide.

While a student at Elon University, Jordan received the Presser Music Scholarship Award, an honor documented by the university in its campus leadership recognition. His broader community service has included church leadership, nonprofit and alumni board service, neighborhood leadership and earlier civic and municipal service.

In Baton Rouge, Jordan has remained connected to music, church, educational, creative and community projects. He is also the builder and maintainer of Thinksday.org, a family publishing project centered on weekly reflections by Nat Tupper.
